*[CPM]: Cost per 1,000 ad impressions
*[CTR]: Share of people who clicked the ad (clicks / impressions)
*[CPC]: Cost per click
*[CPA]: Cost per action or customer
*[CPL]: Cost per lead
*[CR]: Conversion rate: share of visitors who took the target action
*[ROAS]: Revenue for every euro spent on ads
*[ROMI]: Return on ad spend after cost of goods
*[CAPI]: Conversions API — sends events from your site's server to Meta
*[UTM]: Link tags that show where a visitor came from
*[CRM]: Customer and lead management system
*[EMQ]: Event Match Quality in Events Manager
*[UGC]: Content made by ordinary people or creators rather than the brand
*[ABO]: Budget set at ad set level
*[CBO]: Budget set at campaign level
*[GDPR]: EU General Data Protection Regulation
*[DSA]: EU Digital Services Act
*[TTPA]: EU regulation on political advertising
*[VAT]: Value Added Tax
