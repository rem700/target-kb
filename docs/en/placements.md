# New placements

Beyond Feed, Stories and Reels, Meta keeps adding new places where your ads can appear. With Advantage+ placements on, they're included automatically — so it's worth knowing what they are and how they look.

## Threads
Meta's text-based social network, similar to X (Twitter).

- Since 2026, Threads ads are available in all countries. (official)
- Threads is included in Advantage+ placements by default.
- Since autumn 2026 you no longer need an Instagram account to advertise on Threads. (practice)
- What works: short text + an image, like a normal post. Conversational tone.
- Meta doesn't publish averages. Check Threads results in the placement breakdown.

## WhatsApp Status
Ads in WhatsApp's "Updates" tab, between your contacts' statuses. Similar to Instagram Stories.

- The EU launch was delayed over GDPR. In 2026 WhatsApp announced that Status ads and promoted channels are available in all countries, including Europe. (official/media reports)
- Ads don't appear in personal chats or groups.
- Since September 2026, partnership ads can also run in WhatsApp Status. (practice)
- Format — vertical, like Stories: 9:16.

## Click-to-WhatsApp ads
Not a new placement, but a new pricing rule: if someone starts a chat from an ad and you reply in time, the free messaging window is now up to 7 days. More in [Ads that open a chat](messaging.md).

## What was removed
- Messenger Stories (August 2026).
- Instagram Explore — via the API (July 2026).
- Poll ads on Facebook (October 2026).

## How to check whether a new placement works
1. Open a report with the **"By placement"** breakdown ([Reading reports](reports.md)).
2. Compare cost per result across placements.
3. If a placement spends but doesn't deliver, lower its bid with value rules or exclude it. After excluding, turn off "limited spend on excluded placements" ([Non-obvious things](08-hidden.md)).
