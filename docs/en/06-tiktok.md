# TikTok: different rules of the game

## How TikTok differs from Instagram
If Instagram is a shop window, TikTok is a street festival. People came to be entertained, and an ad survives only if it's entertaining too.

- The audience is younger on average.
- Ads must look like normal TikTok videos, not like ads.
- Videos wear out faster than on Instagram, so you need new ones more often.
- TikTok is good at introducing people to you. Instagram more often helps sell to people who are already ready to buy. (practice)

## Setting up the account
1. Create a **TikTok Business Center** — the equivalent of Meta's business portfolio: it holds ad accounts, pixels and access.
2. Inside it, create an ad account in **TikTok Ads Manager**. Choose currency and time zone carefully, as with Meta.
3. In the EU, TikTok may ask for a VAT number. With a valid number, no VAT is added on top of the ad cost (more in [Payment, invoices and taxes](billing.md)). (official)
4. Payment: automatic payments charge when you hit the threshold or on the bill date. Prepaid cards don't work for automatic payments. (official)
5. Connect your TikTok account to the ad account — you need it for Spark Ads and for comments under ads.

## Smart+: the local autopilot
**Smart+** is TikTok's version of Meta's Advantage+. It picks the audience, splits the money, chooses placements and video combinations on its own. (official)

Since 2026 you can turn the autopilot on piece by piece: for example, let the robot handle the audience but choose placements yourself. (official)

What it needs to work well:

- **5–10 videos** in one ad group (practice);
- **a budget from $50 a day** per Smart+ campaign (practice). For product catalog ads, TikTok advises a budget at least 10 times the average cost per customer. (official)

## What the video should be like
- Vertical, 9–30 seconds.
- **Sound matters.** Unlike Instagram, people on TikTok have it on. But businesses can only use trending music from the Commercial Music Library.
- You have 1–2 seconds to hook people.
- A real person in the frame and text on screen.

!!! tip "Spark Ads"
    Instead of a video shot specially for ads, you can promote a normal post that's already published — your own or a creator's (with their permission). Such ads keep their likes and comments and look natural.

!!! example "Anya on TikTok"
    Instead of "Buy a candle" — a video "I spent 3 months learning to make candles, here's what went wrong". People watch the story and at the end see where to buy.

Look for ideas in **TikTok Creative Center**: trends, popular sounds and successful ads.

## Tracking
Same as Meta: **TikTok Pixel** on the site plus **Events API** from the server. The events are similar. TikTok counts results by its own rules, so the only fair way to compare TikTok and Instagram is by real sales.

## TikTok Shop
TikTok Shop is a store inside the app: people see a product in a video and buy it without leaving TikTok.

- Since 15 June 2026 TikTok Shop operates in 10 European countries: Austria, Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Spain and the UK. (official)
- The Sell Across Europe feature lets a seller from one country sell to other countries where Shop operates.
- **Hungary isn't on the list yet.** A Hungarian shop is left with regular ads leading to its website — or selling into countries where Shop exists, if you deliver there.

## Common TikTok problems
**Ad rejected because of the website**
: the page doesn't open on a phone, doesn't match the ad, or lacks contacts and terms. Check your landing page (see [Where the ad leads](landing.md)).

**Sound muted or ad rejected for music**
: the track isn't from the Commercial Music Library. Replace the music.

**Few impressions**
: the budget is too small for Smart+, too few videos, or the videos look like TV commercials. Add native videos.

**A video "deflated" fast**
: on TikTok fatigue sets in faster than on Meta. Keep a stock of new videos.

**Ad rejected for its text or design**
: TikTok's official list includes non-obvious reasons: symbols instead of letters ("S@le"), grammar mistakes, cut-off text in videos, text or logos covered by stickers, third-party logos without permission, exaggerated claims ("No. 1 in the world"). For online shops — no clear return policy on the site.

**The wrong objective**
: TikTok optimizes for exactly what you asked. The "Reach" objective gives lots of cheap views but few buyers. For sales, choose an objective with website conversions.

**A fragmented budget**
: a small budget split across many ad groups means none of them learns. As with Meta, fewer groups is better.

**A Spark Ads code stopped working**
: codes have an expiry chosen by the post's author: 7, 30 or 60 days. Ask the creator for a new code for the period you need.

**Negative comments**
: reply calmly, hide insults. Aggressive comments drag the ad's results down.

## Careful
- Health and medical topics are checked strictly; some are banned outright.
- Music in ads must be licensed.
