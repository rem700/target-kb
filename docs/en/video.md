# Filming videos on your phone

Reels and TikTok ads don't need a studio. They need a phone, window light and an understanding of what makes a video watchable. Here's the minimum that gives 80% of the result.

## Equipment (minimum)
- **A phone** from the last 3–4 years. Use the main (rear) camera, not the selfie one, unless you're filming yourself.
- **Light**: a window during the day. Face the window, don't have it behind you. In the evening — a ring light or any soft lamp in front.
- **Sound**: if you speak on camera, a cheap lavalier mic. People forgive bad picture more easily than bad sound.
- **Support**: a tripod or a stack of books. Shaky footage is tiring.

## Camera settings
- Vertical, **9:16**.
- 1080p or 4K, 30 frames per second (60 for fast movement and slow motion).
- Wipe the lens. Seriously: a blurry picture is the most common mistake.
- Lock focus and exposure (long-press the screen) so the picture doesn't "jump".

## Framing
- Keep the important stuff in the centre. The top and bottom of the screen are covered by Instagram and TikTok text and buttons.
- Close-ups: hands, details, texture, faces. Wide shots get lost on a small screen.
- Change the shot every 2–3 seconds: close → medium → detail. It holds attention.

## What to film: 6 simple formats
1. **Process**: how the product is made or the service is done.
2. **Unboxing**: from the box to the result.
3. **Talking head**: you say something useful to camera.
4. **Before / after** (not for health topics, see [Restrictions](07-restrictions.md)).
5. **Answering a question** from the comments.
6. **A customer review** on camera (with their consent).

## Editing
- Free apps are enough: **CapCut**, Instagram's Edits, your phone's built-in editor.
- Cut pauses and "umms". Keep the pace lively.
- **Subtitles are a must**: most people watch without sound. CapCut makes them automatically — check for mistakes.
- Music only from licensed libraries (Sound Collection for Meta, Commercial Music Library for TikTok).
- The first 1–3 seconds are the strongest: a result, a question, an unexpected shot (see [Creatives](03-creatives.md)).

## One shooting day — a month of videos
Film in batches: in 2–3 hours you can shoot material for 8–10 different videos.

- Prepare a list of 8–10 ideas (use the [creative brief](templates.md)).
- Film each scene with spare takes and different shots.
- Then build different videos from the same footage: different hooks, different order, different length.

!!! example "Anya's example"
    One Sunday Anya filmed: pouring wax, lighting a candle in the dark, packing an order, herself explaining "why soy wax", a friend unboxing a gift. That became 9 videos for the month.

## Pre-publishing checklist
- [ ] Vertical 9:16, clean lens, sharp picture
- [ ] The first 3 seconds hook without sound
- [ ] Subtitles present and correct
- [ ] Nothing important covered by the interface at the top and bottom
- [ ] Licensed music
- [ ] One video — one idea
