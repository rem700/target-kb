# Ads in several languages

One city can be home to people speaking different languages. In Budapest there are Hungarians, expats, students, tourists, Ukrainian and Russian speakers. Which language should your ads use, and how do you avoid confusing the robot?

## The main rule
**One ad — one language.** Andromeda "reads" the creative. If a video has Hungarian subtitles and English copy, it won't know who to show it to. Neither will people.

## How Meta decides a person's language
Meta looks at the language set in the person's account, not where they are. If someone lives in Budapest but uses Instagram in English, they fall into the "English-speaking" audience. (practice)

The audience settings have a **"Languages"** field. Meta recommends using it when your audience speaks a language **that isn't common in that country**. For Hungarians in Hungary you can leave it empty.

## Three working setups
**1. Local language only**
: for businesses serving locals: a salon, delivery, a local service. Creatives in Hungarian, no language set.

**2. Separate ad sets per language**
: one ad set — Hungary with no language set, Hungarian creatives. Another — Hungary + "English", English creatives. A third for Ukrainian or Russian if that's your audience. Plus: everything is transparent. Minus: the budget is split and learning is slower.

**3. One ad with language versions**
: Meta has a multi-language ads feature: you upload versions of the text (and optionally images or video) in different languages, and Meta shows each person the version in their language. Handy when the budget is small and there are several languages.

!!! example "Example"
    A candle workshop in Budapest: the main ad set is in Hungarian. A second, small one is in English for expats and tourists in December (gifts to take home). Each ad set has a landing page in its own language.

## Landing page and chats
- Someone who clicks an English ad should land on an **English** page.
- In chats, reply in the customer's language. If you can't speak a language, don't advertise in it.
- Reviews and prices in the right language too.

## Translation: machine or human
- Meta can automatically translate text, text on images and even video voiceovers. It's fast, but have a native speaker check it.
- Hungarian is a difficult language, and machine translation often sounds unnatural. For your main Hungarian ads, human-written copy is better. (practice)
- Humour and wordplay almost never translate — rewrite them for the language.

## Tourists
If you work with tourists (tours, photo shoots, restaurants), location settings let you pick not only "people living here" but also "recently here" or "travelling here". The language for tourists is usually English or their home country's language.
