# Anya's campaign from start to finish

One example from setup to results over two months. Anya and her candle workshop are made up and the numbers are illustrative, but the mistakes and decisions are typical. The point is to show how the sections of this base fit together into real work.

## Starting point
- Handmade candles, a WordPress + WooCommerce site, delivery across Hungary.
- Average order 35 €, cost including delivery 14 €, profit per order 21 €.
- Anya is willing to spend up to half the profit on ads: **a customer must cost no more than ~10 €**.
- Test budget: 900 € for a month (30 € a day).

## Week 0. Preparation
Anya goes through [setting up from scratch](setup.md): portfolio, Page, Instagram, ad account in euros, pixel via a plugin, CAPI, domain verification.

**Mistake #1.** The pixel was installed, but the cookie banner blocked it for everyone who didn't click "Accept". In "Test events" a purchase only showed up for Anya herself. Connecting CAPI got the events flowing.

**Landing page.** Instead of the homepage, she made a "Gift sets" page with the price and delivery on the first screen.

## Week 1. Launch
- "Sales" campaign, Advantage+, broad audience: Hungary, 22–60.
- One ad set, 30 € a day, optimizing for purchases.
- Four different concepts: "cosy evening", "gift for a colleague under 40 €", "how I make candles" (process), a customer review.

**Mistake #2.** She left location expansion on — some impressions went to Austria, where she doesn't deliver. She unticked it and checked the region breakdown.

**Week result:** 210 € spent, 14 purchases → 15 € per customer. Above target, status "Learning".

## Week 2. Patience
Anya leaves the campaign alone. She sees that "gift for a colleague" gets the most budget and purchases, while "process" barely spends.

**Result:** 210 €, 19 purchases → 11 €. Status "Learning Limited" — 50 purchases a week isn't reachable on this budget. The price is acceptable, so Anya decides to live with that status (see [case 6](09-problem-cases.md)).

## Week 3. New ideas
In the comments Anya keeps seeing one question: "How long does it burn?" She makes a new video: "This candle burns for 60 hours. I checked" — a time-lapse of a burning candle.

She runs it in a **separate test campaign** at 10 € a day so as not to disturb the main one.

**Mistake #3.** She almost made 5 versions of the same video with different text colours. Remembering how similar ads get merged ([non-obvious things](08-hidden.md)), she made a different concept instead — "gift unboxing".

**Result:** "60 hours" — customers at 8 €. She moves it into the main campaign using the post ID.

## Week 4. First month summary
| | Value |
|---|---|
| Spent | 900 € |
| Purchases (Ads Manager) | 78 |
| Purchases (WooCommerce, with ad UTMs) | 71 |
| Cost per customer (by shop data) | 12.7 € |
| Revenue | 2,485 € |

Cost per customer is a bit above target. But 12 customers ordered again within the month — so a customer brings more over time than a single order.

## Month 2. Growth
- Main campaign budget: +20% every 3 days, from 30 to 50 € a day.
- Every 2 weeks — 2 new concepts. One is filmed by a local creator and run as a **partnership ad**.
- In November impression prices rose ([case 10](09-problem-cases.md)). Anya doesn't panic and adds an offer: "free gift wrapping until 20 December".

**Second month result:** 1,400 €, 128 purchases by shop data → 10.9 € per customer.

## Lessons learned
1. Check tracking before launch, not after.
2. Don't touch the campaign in the first week, however nervous you are.
3. Video ideas are sitting in customers' comments.
4. Test new ideas separately, not by editing a working campaign.
5. Trust your shop's data, not just Ads Manager.
