# Copywriting formulas

Ad copy doesn't need to be a masterpiece. It should continue the video's idea and nudge towards one action. Formulas help you avoid starting from a blank page.

## Five rules before any formula
1. **The first line matters most.** Only 1–2 lines show before "more".
2. **About the person, not about you.** "An evening when you want quiet" beats "We're a workshop with 5 years of experience".
3. **Be specific.** "Burns for 60 hours" beats "burns for a long time".
4. **One action.** "Order on the website" — not "order, follow, message and share".
5. **No personal attributes** ("Struggling with insomnia?") — it breaks the rules (see [Restrictions](07-restrictions.md)).

## AIDA: attention → interest → desire → action
The classic for most ads.

!!! example "Anya's example"
    **Attention:** A gift that won't end up in a cupboard.
    **Interest:** A handmade soy wax candle that burns for 60 hours.
    **Desire:** A pine and orange scent that turns an ordinary evening into a celebration.
    **Action:** Order by 20 December — gift wrapping is free.

## PAS: problem → agitate → solution
Good for services and products that solve a clear pain. Describe the problem as a situation, not as the person's condition.

!!! example "Example"
    **Problem:** Not sure what to get your colleagues again?
    **Agitate:** Chocolate is boring, and a gift card looks like "didn't have time to think".
    **Solution:** A set of three mini candles for 25 € — pretty, useful and looks thoughtful.

## Before → After → Bridge
Show how it was, how it could be, and what gets you there.

!!! example "Example"
    **Before:** An after-work evening lit by a screen.
    **After:** Warm light, a scent and half an hour of quiet.
    **Bridge:** The "Quiet Evening" candle — 30 €, delivery in 2 days.

## The 4U formula for headlines
A good headline is usually:

- **useful** (what do I get?),
- **unique** (why you and not others?),
- **ultra-specific** (numbers, details),
- **urgent** (why now?) — only if the urgency is real.

You don't need all four: two or three are enough.

## Short format
For Reels and TikTok, the caption is often secondary. One line plus a call to action is enough:

- "Burns for 60 hours. Tested. Link in bio."
- "Three scents, one set, 45 €. Delivery across Hungary."

## How to check your copy
- Read it aloud. Does it sound like a person or a press release?
- Remove every word that doesn't change the meaning.
- Give 3–5 text variants in the ad — Meta will find the best combinations.
