# What's new: changelog

Platforms change things all the time, and what worked a year ago may not work now. This page records what changed, when, and what it means in practice. Newest at the top.

Tags: (official) announced by the platform itself; (unconfirmed) reported by specialists, no official confirmation.

## 2026
- **October, Meta.** Poll ads on Facebook removed.
- **September, Meta** (unconfirmed). The free messaging window after WhatsApp ads grew to 7 days. You can now advertise on Threads without an Instagram account. Ads appeared in Instagram live streams.
- **August, Meta.** The Messenger Stories placement removed.
- **July, Meta.** The Instagram Explore placement removed (via the API).
- **May, TikTok.** The Smart+ autopilot can be turned on in parts: separately for audience, budget, placements. (official)
- **3 March, Meta.** A "click" in reports now means only a link click. Likes and saves moved to a separate column. **What it means:** reports will show fewer purchases "from clicks"; check against real sales. (official)
- **January, Meta.** Threads ads available in all countries.
- **15 January, Meta.** Some interests for audience setup were merged or removed. **What it means:** check old saved audiences.
- **12 January, Meta.** The long "after viewing" counting windows (7 and 28 days) were removed. (official)
- **Early in the year, Meta.** The Advantage+ autopilot is on by default for sales, leads and apps. (official)
- **Throughout the year, Meta.** The Andromeda system works in all campaigns. **What it means:** videos decide who sees the ad.

## 2025
- **October, Meta.** Political and social issue ads stopped in the EU.
- **Throughout the year, Meta.** Restrictions for health advertisers: the robot doesn't learn from leads and purchases, especially in the EU.
- **31 March, Meta.** You can no longer exclude people by interests. Only by your own lists. (official)
- **February, Meta.** The Advantage+ autopilot for lead generation appeared.
