# Organic posts and ads

Regular posts (organic) and ads aren't two separate worlds. They work best together: organic shows what lands, ads give it reach.

## Organic as a free testing ground
You're already posting Reels and posts. Watch which of them:

- get watched to the end;
- get saved and shared;
- get comments with questions ("how much?", "where can I buy?").

Those posts are the first candidates for ads. You already know they hook real people.

!!! example "Anya's example"
    A regular Reel, "how I pour the wax", unexpectedly got five times her usual views and lots of "where can I buy" questions. Anya ran it as an ad — and it became one of her best ads.

## How to turn a post into an ad properly
- **Not with the Boost button**, but through Ads Manager: choose "Use existing post". That gives you a proper objective, tracking and control.
- The post's likes, comments and saves **carry over** into the ad. That's social proof: people trust an ad with a lively discussion more.
- On TikTok the same thing is done with **Spark Ads**.

## Ads help organic
- People who see an ad visit your profile. If the profile is lively and useful, some follow and later buy without ads.
- So the profile is part of the ad too (see [Where the ad leads](landing.md)).

## Comments under ads
Ads get comments — good and bad.

- **Answer questions** quickly and to the point: everyone who sees the ad sees the answer.
- **Don't delete honest criticism**; reply calmly instead. Deleting looks suspicious.
- **Hide spam and insults**: Meta Business Suite can automatically hide comments containing certain words.

## Creators and collaborations
A creator's post about your product can be run as a **partnership ad**: it shows under two accounts and, according to Meta, usually performs better than regular ads (see [Non-obvious things](08-hidden.md)). Always with a "Paid partnership" label (see [Law and transparency](legal.md)).

## What not to do
- Don't promote everything: posts that didn't land organically are rarely saved by ads.
- Don't turn your feed into wall-to-wall ads: followers come for content, not a price list.
