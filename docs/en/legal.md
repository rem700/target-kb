# Law and transparency

Legal points that affect almost any ad in the EU. This isn't legal advice: rules differ by country, and in disputed cases it's better to check with a lawyer.

## Ads with creators: the "Paid partnership" label
If a creator promotes your product for money, barter or a gift, it must be clearly labelled.

- Instagram's branded content policies require the creator to use the **"Paid partnership"** label and tag the brand. It works in Feed, Stories, Reels and Live.
- Gifts count too: if a product was given free in exchange for a post, many countries treat it as advertising. (practice)
- The platform label may not fully satisfy the law. In some countries regulators also expect an explicit word like "ad" in the text. (practice)
- Responsibility can fall on the brand, not just the creator. Put labelling in the contract.

If you run a creator's post as an ad, use the **partnership ad** format. Specialists report that in 2026 Meta made it mandatory for branded content in paid ads. (not officially confirmed)

## Ad transparency in the EU (DSA)
Under the EU Digital Services Act, large platforms must keep a public ad archive.

- Any ad shown in the EU can be found in the **Meta Ad Library**. It shows who ran it and who paid, the main targeting settings (age, gender, locations) and approximate reach.
- These ads stay in the library for **a year** after their last impression.
- Practical takeaway: competitors can see your ads and settings. And you can see theirs — a good source of ideas.

## Political and social issue ads
Since October 2025 Meta doesn't run political, electoral or social issue ads in the EU because of the TTPA regulation. If your ad touches on sensitive social topics, it may be rejected even if you're not selling anything political.

## Personal data and cookies (GDPR)
- The pixel and other trackers on your site collect data. In the EU you need a **cookie consent banner**, and the pixel mustn't fire before consent.
- A link to your privacy policy on the site and in Meta instant forms is mandatory.
- Leads from forms are personal data. Store them carefully and don't forward them through open chats.
- Medical data must never reach Meta (see [Restrictions](07-restrictions.md)).

## Discounts and prices
EU rules govern how discounts are shown: if you write "−30%", the discount should generally be calculated from the **lowest price in the 30 days** before the promotion, not from an invented "old" price. (practice, EU price indication rules)

## Reviews
- You can't publish made-up reviews or pass off bought ones as genuine.
- Reviews in ads must be real and used with the author's consent, especially if their name or photo is visible.

## AI
- Meta may label realistic AI-generated images or videos of people and events as made with AI.
- Don't use generated faces posing as real customers or "reviews".
- You can't use a real person's face or voice in an ad without their consent.
