# Payment, invoices and taxes

Money is the most boring part of advertising — and the most unpleasant when something goes wrong. Here's how Meta takes payment, what to do with invoices and why your VAT number matters.

!!! warning "Not tax advice"
    This is the general EU principle. How exactly to record expenses in your country and tax regime is a question for your accountant.

## How Meta charges you
There are two main options (availability depends on country and currency):

**Automatic payments (postpay)**
: ads run "on credit", and Meta charges your card when you reach the billing threshold or on the monthly billing date — whichever comes first. The threshold grows gradually if payments go through without problems.

**Manual payments (prepay)**
: you top up a balance in advance, and Meta spends it as ads run. When the money runs out, ads stop.

!!! example "Anya's example"
    Anya has automatic payments with a 25 € threshold. While it's small, her card is charged 25 € several times a week. After a couple of months without failures the threshold grew, and charges became less frequent.

## Budget, limit and threshold are different things
- **Budget** — how much a campaign spends per day or over its lifetime.
- **Spending limit** — a ceiling for a campaign or the whole account. Hit it and everything stops.
- **Billing threshold** — the amount at which Meta charges your card.

## Common payment problems
- **Payment failed**: not enough money, expired card, the bank blocked a foreign payment. Ads stop until the balance is paid.
- **Prepaid cards** aren't officially supported by Meta: they sometimes work for small amounts, but don't count on them.
- **Many failed payments in a row** are one of the risk signals that can lead to account restrictions.

!!! tip "Tip"
    Allow foreign online payments and recurring charges with your bank in advance. And keep a buffer on the card.

## Invoices and VAT
For businesses in the EU, invoices are issued by **Meta Platforms Ireland Limited**.

- If you've entered a **valid VAT number** in your business details, Meta doesn't add VAT to the invoice. Instead the invoice says something like "reverse charge", and you account for the VAT yourself in your return.
- If there's no VAT number, or Meta couldn't validate it (via the EU's VIES database), VAT is added at your country's rate, as for an ordinary consumer.
- If your business is VAT-exempt, the reverse charge may work differently and that VAT may not be reclaimable. Ask your accountant.

**Where to get invoices:** Ads Manager → "All tools" menu → "Billing & payments" → "Payment activity". You can download an invoice for every payment.

## TikTok
Similar logic. When you create an ad account in the EU, TikTok may ask you to confirm your business status with a VAT number. With a valid number, no VAT is added on top of the ad cost. Automatic payments charge when you hit the threshold or on the bill date; prepaid cards don't work for automatic payments.

## Checklist
- [ ] Business details include name, address and VAT number
- [ ] Your bank allows foreign online payments
- [ ] An account spending limit is set (protection against mistakes and hacks)
- [ ] Invoices are downloaded monthly and passed to your accountant
