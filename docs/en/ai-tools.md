# AI in practice

AI saves hours of work, but it can make your ads look just like your competitors'. Here's how to use it well.

## What Meta can do (Advantage+ creative)
The set depends on your account, but generally there's:

- **Text variations** — Meta suggests its own headlines and primary text based on yours.
- **Background generation** and **image expansion** to fit other formats.
- **Video from images** — several photos become a video with motion, overlays and music.
- **AI music** for videos.
- **Translation** of text and text on images, and **dubbing** of voiceovers into other languages.
- **Brand settings** — logo, fonts, colours, tone and banned words, so generation matches your style.

### What to turn on
| Feature | Advice |
|---|---|
| Text variations | Fine to turn on, but reread every version: promises, errors, tone |
| Image expansion | Useful: one photo fits 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 |
| Video from images | Good for a quick test if you have no video. Real footage usually performs better |
| Translation and dubbing | Only with a native speaker's check ([Ads in several languages](multilingual.md)) |
| Generated people | Careful: you can't pass off generated people as real customers ([Law and transparency](legal.md)) |

!!! tip "Before launch"
    Open the preview and look at what Meta generated. Creative enhancements sometimes switch themselves on — check them ([Non-obvious things](08-hidden.md)).

## ChatGPT, Claude and others: what they're good for
- **Hook ideas**: "Give me 20 opening lines for a video about handmade candles, audience — women 25–45, motive — a gift."
- **Copy variants by formula**: AIDA, PAS ([Copywriting formulas](copywriting.md)).
- **Objection handling**: "Why might someone not buy a 30 € candle? How do I address it in an ad?"
- **A brief and a video script** using the [template](templates.md).
- **Report analysis**: export a table from Ads Manager and ask it to find weak ads and the reasons.
- **Policy check**: "Does this copy address personal attributes or promise results?"

## How to avoid a "plastic" result
- **Give context**: who the customer is, what words they use (take them from reviews and comments), what your tone is.
- **Ask for many versions** and pick the best, rather than taking the first.
- **Edit it to sound like you**: cut stock phrases like "discover" or "the perfect solution".
- **Check facts yourself**: AI can invent numbers, reviews and specs.
- **Don't upload** customers' personal data or medical information to chatbots.

## Where AI won't help
- Working out **what really makes you different** — only you know that.
- Filming **real videos** with real people — your main advantage over competitors who generate everything.
- Replying to a customer in a chat in a way that makes them buy. A template — yes; a real conversation — you.
