# Where the ad leads: site and profile

The ad's job is to get the click. Whether the person buys depends on what they see after it. A great ad leading to a poor page is like a beautiful sign above a locked door.

## The "promise = delivery" rule
Someone clicked on a video "gift set of 3 candles for 45 €". The page must **immediately** show that set and that price. If they land on a homepage with 40 products and have to search for the set themselves, half of them leave.

!!! example "Anya's example"
    Anya was sending set ads to her homepage. Then she made a separate "Gift sets" page with the same photo as in the ad. Clicks stayed the same, purchases almost doubled.

## Page checklist
**Speed**
: the page opens on a phone in 2–3 seconds. Heavy images and dozens of plugins are the main enemies.

**First screen on a phone**
: without scrolling you can see what it is, what it costs, and the action button. Almost all social traffic is on phones.

**One main action**
: "Buy", "Book" or "Message us". Not five equally sized buttons.

**Price and terms**
: price, delivery, timing, returns. A hidden price scares people off.

**Trust**
: reviews with photos, real photos of the product or process, contacts, clear terms.

**Short form**
: only the fields you need. Every extra field reduces sign-ups.

**Language**
: the page is in the same language as the ad.

## If you send people to your Instagram profile
The profile is a landing page too. People arrive from an ad and decide in a couple of seconds whether this is a "living" business.

- **Bio**: what you do, for whom, where. One sentence.
- **Link**: to the right page or a messenger, not to everything at once.
- **Pinned posts**: best review, main product, how to order.
- **Highlights**: prices, reviews, directions, FAQ.
- **Recent posts**: if the last post was six months ago, trust drops.

## How to tell the page is the problem
- Good CTR in Ads Manager, but few purchases or leads.
- In Google Analytics, a high bounce rate and short time on page for ad traffic.
- Ask a friend to open the page on a phone and find how to buy. Time them.
