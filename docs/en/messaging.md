# Ads that open a chat

Instead of a site or form, the ad opens a chat: Instagram Direct, Messenger or WhatsApp. For services where customers need to clarify something (date, size, price for their case), this is often the best option: it's easier to send a message than fill in a form.

## How to set it up
- Campaign objective: **Engagement** or **Leads** with "Messaging apps" as the conversion location.
- Choose where to send people: Instagram Direct, Messenger, WhatsApp, or several.
- Set up a **greeting** and **quick question** buttons: "How much is it?", "Is this date free?", "Where are you?". Tapping a button is easier than inventing a first message.

## WhatsApp: the free window
If a customer starts a chat with your WhatsApp Business from an ad, a **free window** opens: messages in that conversation aren't charged. The standard window is 72 hours. (official)

According to WhatsApp Business service providers, since 28 September 2026 the window is up to 7 days. (not officially confirmed)

Separately, the 24-hour rule applies: if the customer hasn't written to you for over a day, you can only start with a pre-approved message template.

!!! tip "What this means in practice"
    This applies to businesses on the WhatsApp Business Platform (via the API or services like Wati, SleekFlow). In the regular WhatsApp Business app, chats are free anyway.

## The main rule: speed
Someone who messaged from an ad is holding their phone right now. An hour later they're chatting with a competitor.

- Aim to reply within **5 minutes** during working hours.
- Turn on an **auto-reply** that gives something useful right away: a price, a link, the next step. Not just "We'll reply soon".
- Outside working hours, the auto-reply says honestly when you'll answer.

## First reply script
A good first reply: greet, answer the question, ask **one** clarifying question.

!!! example "Anya's example"
    Customer: "How much is it?"

    Bad: "Hello! Prices depend on many factors. What are you interested in?"

    Good: "Hi! Candles are 15 to 30 €, a gift set of three is 45 €. Is it for you or a gift? I'll help you pick a scent 🙂"

## "How much?" — and silence
The most common situation. What helps:

- Give the price right away instead of hiding it. Those who find it too expensive will leave anyway, and you don't annoy the rest.
- End your message with a question that's easy to answer.
- One polite follow-up a day later: "Did you manage to choose? I can hold the set until Friday".
- No more than one or two reminders.

## How to measure results
- How many chats started (visible in Ads Manager).
- How many became sales (only you can see this: keep simple records or a CRM).
- Cost per chat and cost per customer. Cheap chats without sales aren't a success.
