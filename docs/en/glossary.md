# Glossary

All the terms from this base in one place, alphabetically. Across the base you can hover over (or tap on a phone) any abbreviation to see a tooltip.

**A/B test** — comparing two versions that differ in one detail.

**ABO** — budget set at ad set level.

**Ad set** — the middle level of an ad: audience, placements, budget.

**Advantage+** — Meta's set of automatic settings: picks the audience, placements, budget and creative variations.

**Andromeda** — Meta's system that chooses which ad to show to whom; it relies mostly on the creative itself.

**Attribution** — the rules for crediting a purchase to an ad.

**Audience segments** — labelling "new / engaged / existing customers" for reports.

**Billing threshold** — the amount at which Meta charges your card.

**Broad audience** — only country, age and gender; the robot picks the rest.

**Business portfolio** — the "folder" in Meta that holds Pages, Instagram, ad accounts and access.

**Campaign** — the top level: the ad's objective.

**CAPI (Conversions API)** — sending events to Meta from your site's server, complementing the pixel.

**CBO / Advantage+ Campaign Budget** — budget at campaign level; Meta splits it between ad sets.

**Conversion Leads** — an optimization goal for leads that became customers according to your CRM.

**CPA** — cost per action or customer.

**CPC** — cost per click.

**CPL** — cost per lead.

**CPM** — cost per 1,000 impressions.

**CR** — conversion rate: share of people who took the target action.

**Creative** — the image or video in an ad.

**CRM** — a customer and lead management system.

**CTR** — share of viewers who clicked.

**Deduplication** — protection against double-counting an event from the pixel and CAPI.

**EMQ** — Event Match Quality: how well Meta recognises people from your data.

**Engage-through** — purchases after a like, save or other interaction without a link click.

**Entity ID** — an unofficial name for a group of similar ads that Andromeda treats as one.

**Fatigue** — performance drop when the audience is tired of an ad.

**Frequency** — how many times on average a person has seen the ad.

**Hook rate** — share of people who watched the first 3 seconds of a video.

**Instant form** — a lead form inside Facebook or Instagram.

**Lead** — a request, a potential customer's contact details.

**Learning** — the first days of an ad set, while the robot works out who to show it to.

**Learning Limited** — the status of an ad set that doesn't reach about 50 target actions in 7 days.

**Lookalike (LAL)** — people similar to your customers.

**Offer** — a specific deal: product + terms.

**Optimization** — the action you ask the robot to look for.

**Partnership ad** — an ad shown under two accounts, e.g. a brand and a creator.

**Pixel** — code on your site that records visitors' actions.

**Placement** — where an ad is shown.

**Retargeting** — ads for people who already visited your site or profile.

**ROAS** — revenue per euro spent on ads.

**ROMI** — return on ad spend after cost of goods.

**Smart+** — TikTok's automation, similar to Advantage+.

**Spark Ads** — TikTok ads based on an already published post.

**UGC** — content made by ordinary people or creators.

**Unit economics** — working out how much you can spend per customer and stay profitable.

**UTM** — link tags that show where a visit came from.

**Value rules** — a way to raise or lower bids for specific groups of people.
