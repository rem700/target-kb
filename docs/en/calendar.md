# Seasonal calendar

Ad prices change over the year. Before big holidays everyone advertises and impressions get pricier; afterwards they get cheaper. Knowing this in advance means less panic and less overpaying.

!!! tip "General rule"
    Launch seasonal campaigns 3–6 weeks ahead: learning and tests take time. Launching "three days before the holiday" is expensive and stressful.

## By month (Europe)
| Month | What's happening | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **January** | Ads get cheaper after the holidays, people spend less | A good time to test new ideas cheaply, clearance sales |
| **February** | Valentine's Day (14.02) | Gifts for couples: launch in mid-January |
| **March** | International Women's Day, 8 March (important in Hungary, Ukraine, Eastern Europe) | Gifts for women: launch in early February |
| **March–April** | Easter (date varies) | Decor, gifts, family products |
| **May** | Mother's Day (in Hungary, the first Sunday of May) | Gifts for mums: launch in mid-April |
| **June–August** | Holidays, tourism, weddings | Tourist and seasonal services; demand is lower for many other niches |
| **Late August–September** | Back to work and school | Courses, services, home goods |
| **October** | Getting ready for Q4, Halloween | **Launch and train holiday campaigns** |
| **November** | Black Friday (last Friday of November), Cyber Monday | The most expensive impressions of the year. Strong offers, budget with a buffer |
| **December** | Christmas and New Year | Gifts. Mind delivery times: after ~15–18 December, sales needing delivery drop |
| **Late December** | After Christmas | Impressions get cheaper; good for gift cards and "treat yourself" |

## How to plan
1. Mark the 3–5 most important dates for your business in a calendar.
2. 6 weeks before — come up with the offer and creatives.
3. 4 weeks before — launch and learning.
4. Budget for the cost per result being noticeably higher than usual in November–December.
5. After the season, write down what worked (see [Templates](templates.md)).
