# Restrictions: where you can trip up

## The main rule: don't "read people's minds"
An ad mustn't act as if it knows something personal about the viewer: their health, weight, money, religion, orientation.

| Not allowed | Allowed |
|---|---|
| "Does your knee hurt?" | "Rehabilitation after knee injuries" |
| "Tired of debt?" | "How to plan a monthly budget" |
| "Put on weight over the holidays?" | "A January workout programme" |

The difference: the first phrase addresses a specific person and their problem, the second simply describes a service.

## Health and medicine: special mode
Since 2025 Meta treats health advertisers (clinics, doctors, some health products) more strictly, especially in Europe.

**What can be restricted**

- The robot can't learn from leads and purchases. It only sees that people opened the site.
- Some link data (tags, parameters) isn't passed on.
- Audiences and events whose names hint at a condition ("arthritis", "doctor appointment") get blocked.

Important: **you can still advertise** — the robot just learns worse.

**What to do**

- Check your status in Events Manager. A red icon next to your site means a restriction.
- Never put the pixel in a patient portal and never send medical data.
- Name your own events neutrally, with no medical meaning. Simply renaming a blocked event doesn't always help.
- Collect leads right inside Instagram/Facebook (forms or chats). Then the robot sees the result within the platform.
- A broad audience and strong videos.
- Don't promise treatment results and don't show before-and-after.

## Special categories
If an ad is about **credit and finance, jobs, housing, politics or social issues**, it must be marked as a special category. Then you can't choose age, gender or narrow locations — this protects against discrimination.

Since October 2025 Meta doesn't show political and social issue ads in the EU at all (because of the TTPA regulation).

## European specifics
- **GDPR and cookies.** If someone doesn't accept cookies, the pixel can't see them. Some purchases simply won't appear in reports.
- EU users can choose less personalized ads, which leaves the robot with less data.

## If an ad was rejected or the account blocked
Common rejection reasons:

- promising results ("lose 10 kg");
- before-and-after photos;
- addressing personal attributes (see above);
- other brands and unlicensed music;
- the ad promises one thing and the site shows another.

**What to do:**

- Ad rejected → fix it and resubmit, or request another review in "Account Quality".
- Account blocked → appeal in the same place.

!!! warning "Don't create a new ad account to get around a block"
    Meta links accounts together, and the ban will spread to the new ones.

**How to protect yourself in advance:** two-factor authentication for all admins, a verified business, a backup admin, no sudden budget jumps on a new account.

## Music
In Meta ads, use only music from Sound Collection (Meta's free library) or a licence you bought. A popular track from regular Reels in an ad can lead to rejection or muted sound.
