# Choosing a platform

Strictly speaking, targeted ads means social media ads. But it's worth choosing with the neighbouring options in mind. The key question: **is the person already looking for what you sell, or not yet?**

## Two logics
**Demand already exists** (someone searches "prom dress rental Budapest")
: search works best: Google Ads. You show up at the moment they're ready.

**No demand yet** (someone wasn't thinking about candles, saw one — and wanted it)
: social media works: Meta, TikTok. You create the desire.

Many businesses use both: social to get discovered, search to catch those already looking.

## Comparison
| Platform | When it fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram and Facebook (Meta)** | Almost any consumer business: products, services, local business | The most developed ads and tracking, a broad audience. This base is mostly about it |
| **TikTok** | Younger audience, emotional purchases, anything that shows well in a short video | Needs lots of native videos; creatives wear out fast (see [TikTok](06-tiktok.md)) |
| **Google Search** | Clear existing demand: services "near me", urgent needs, B2B | Clicks cost more: according to WordStream, the average Google CPC is more than double Meta's. But intent is higher |
| **YouTube** | Explaining a complex product, educational content | Long videos, pricier production |
| **Pinterest** | Interiors, fashion, weddings, recipes, DIY | People plan purchases ahead |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B, hiring, expensive business services | Expensive clicks, but precise targeting by job title and industry |

## How to choose your first platform
1. Do people search for your product on Google? Check in Keyword Planner, or just start typing the query and look at the suggestions.
2. Where do your customers spend time? Ask 5–10 current customers.
3. Can you regularly make short videos? If not, TikTok will be hard.
4. Start with **one** platform and get it to a stable result. Spreading a small budget over three at once is a common mistake.

!!! example "Anya's example"
    People do search "scented candle" on Google, but the competition with big shops is huge. Candles, though, look great on video. Anya starts with Meta, and a couple of months later tries TikTok with videos about how they're made.
