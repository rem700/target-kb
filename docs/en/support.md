# Meta support, identity checks and outages

Sooner or later something breaks: the account is blocked, money is charged oddly, ads behave strangely. And it turns out there's nowhere to call. Here's what actually works.

## Where to get help
For anything about ads, ad accounts and billing, Meta sends advertisers to **Meta Business Support Home**, not the general Help Center.

**Meta's AI assistant**
: free for all advertisers. It answers right away, troubleshoots problems and ad results, and hands you to a human when needed. In practice it often repeats what's in the help articles — but it's the place to start.

**Chat with an ads specialist**
: the fastest way to reach a real person. Not available to everyone: only "eligible" business advertisers and not in every country. The button usually appears in the Ads Manager help panel: "Can't find what you're looking for?" → "Contact support".

**Meta Verified for business**
: a paid subscription; among other things it gives email and chat support for account issues.

**Requesting a review in Account Quality**
: the main route for rejected ads and blocked accounts (see [case 4](09-problem-cases.md)).

!!! warning "There's no Meta support phone number"
    The internet is full of "Meta support numbers", pages and "Meta Support" accounts offering to unblock your account. They're scammers after your passwords, codes and money. Only go through your own Ads Manager or business.facebook.com.

### How to write to support
- One issue — one request.
- Include the ad account, campaign or ad ID.
- Attach screenshots: what you see, when it started, what you've tried.
- Write calmly and briefly. If a bot answers, ask for your request to be passed to a human.
- Keep the case number.

## Identity checks
When Meta doubts that a real person runs the account, it may ask you to confirm your identity. This most often happens during an appeal after a block, when logging in from a new device, or after suspicious activity.

- Usually they ask for a **document** (passport, ID card, driving licence) or a **video selfie**: a short video turning your head.
- The name on the document must match the name on your Facebook profile. If your profile uses a nickname or a different spelling, the check may fail.
- Only complete checks inside Facebook or Ads Manager, not via links in emails or messages.
- A check can take from a few hours to several days. Resubmitting the same data usually doesn't speed it up.
- You have 180 days to appeal a blocked account — don't wait.

!!! tip "How to protect yourself in advance"
    A profile in your real name, a second portfolio admin, a verified business, two-factor authentication (see [Setting up from scratch](setup.md)). Then one profile being blocked won't cut you off from your business.

## When Meta itself breaks
Sometimes it's not you but the platform: reports don't update, the budget is spent in an hour, impressions drop for everyone. Outages happen regularly: by one count there were more than 60 Meta Ads outages from October 2024 to March 2026, and they became noticeably more frequent by late 2025.

### How to tell it's an outage
- Check Meta's business status page: metastatus.com.
- See whether others are complaining: r/FacebookAds on Reddit, industry chats.
- Signs of an outage: strange behaviour in all campaigns at once although you changed nothing; zeros in reports while sales are coming in; interface errors.

### What to do
1. **Don't edit anything.** Edits during an outage only restart learning, and afterwards you won't know what helped.
2. If the budget is "burning" with no results — **pause** the campaign or lower the spending limit instead of changing settings.
3. **Save evidence**: screenshots with date and time, spend, campaign IDs.
4. Contact support and ask for a credit for wasted spend. Honestly: refunds are hard to get, but without evidence they're almost impossible.
5. After an outage, give campaigns 2–3 days to settle before drawing conclusions.

### When AI generation goes wrong
Advertisers complain that Meta's AI features sometimes create strange, off-brand ads. If that happens, turn off creative enhancements for those ads and check the defaults (see [Non-obvious things](08-hidden.md) and [AI in practice](ai-tools.md)).
