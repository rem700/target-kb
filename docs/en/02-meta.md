# Meta Ads Manager: launching an ad

Meta Ads Manager is like an aeroplane cockpit: a hundred buttons, and you only need about ten. Here they are.

## Step 1. Choose the objective
The objective tells the robot what success means for you. There are six:

| Objective | When to choose it | Anya's example |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | you just want as many people as possible to see you | "let the whole city know these candles exist" |
| **Traffic** | you need visits to the site or profile | gather people to show them another ad later |
| **Engagement** | you need DMs/WhatsApp messages, views, likes | "message me and I'll help you pick a scent" |
| **Leads** | you need contacts: requests, calls, forms | sign-ups for a candle-making workshop |
| **App promotion** | you need app installs | — |
| **Sales** | you need purchases on the site | sell candles before Christmas |

!!! warning "A common mistake"
    Choosing "Traffic" when you actually need sales. The robot will honestly bring people who click, but clicking and buying are different habits. Ask for what you really need.

## Step 2. Turn on the autopilot (Advantage+)
Since 2026 Meta turns on **Advantage+** by default — a set of automatic settings. It decides who sees the ad, where, and how to split the money. (official)

Sounds a bit scary, but in practice the robot almost always does better than manual setup. It has data on billions of people; you have guesses.

## Step 3. Audience: hints, not orders
Setting up the audience used to be a specialist's main skill: "women, 28–40, interests: yoga, aromatherapy, cosiness". Now all of that is just a **suggestion**. The robot can go beyond it if it finds buyers elsewhere.

What's still strict (the robot won't break it):

- country or city;
- minimum age;
- language;
- excluding your own lists (for example, "don't show to people who already bought").

!!! tip "What a beginner should do"
    Choose the country and age, leave everything else empty. That's called a **broad audience**, and in 2026 it's the standard.

A couple more facts:

- **Retargeting** often doesn't need its own campaign now: the autopilot already shows ads to people who visited.
- You can no longer exclude people by interests (since March 2025). Only by your own lists. (official)

## Step 4. Structure: don't split it up
Beginners love making ten ad sets: "women 25–30", "women 30–35", "men buying gifts"… Each set gets a little money, and the robot doesn't properly learn anywhere.

!!! example "A simple setup for Anya"
    - **Main campaign**: 1 ad set, broad audience, 3–6 different ads. Most of the money goes here.
    - **Test campaign**: small budget. Anya tries new videos here. The best ones move to the main campaign.

## Step 5. Money
- **Campaign budget** (Advantage+ Campaign Budget): Meta splits the money between ad sets itself. Fits almost always.
- **Ad set budget**: handy for tests, when you want every version to definitely get money.
- A **daily budget** suits ongoing ads; a **lifetime budget** suits a promotion with an end date.

How to pay for results (bid strategy): at the start always choose **"Highest volume"**. Other strategies (cost limits, etc.) make sense once you have some data.

## Step 6. Where to show ads
Keep **all placements** (Advantage+ placements): it's usually cheaper. But then prepare videos in different formats:

- **9:16** — vertical, full screen (Reels, Stories);
- **4:5** — almost square (feed);
- **1:1** — square, as a backup.

## For services: leads and chats
If you sell a service, two options work well:

- **A form right inside Instagram/Facebook.** People don't leave for your site, and their name and phone fill in automatically. "More volume" gives lots of cheap leads, but many are random. "Higher intent" adds a confirmation step, and the leads are better.
- **Ads with a "Send message" button.** People land straight in Direct or WhatsApp. Works only if you reply fast.

## Checklist before you hit "Publish"
- [ ] Tracking works: Meta sees purchases or leads (see "Tracking").
- [ ] Links have UTM parameters.
- [ ] Existing customers are excluded, if the goal is new ones.
- [ ] There are 3–6 different ads in vertical and almost-square formats.
- [ ] Link, form and phone checked with your own hands.
- [ ] The budget isn't below the minimum (see "Basics and numbers").
- [ ] Promise yourself not to touch anything for 3–7 days.
