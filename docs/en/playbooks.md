# Playbooks by business type

The general rules are the same, but an online shop and a beauty salon launch ads differently. Here are ready starting points — not strict instructions, but "where to begin if you don't know where to begin".

## Online shop
**Objective**: Sales (Advantage+ Sales).
**Send people to**: a product or collection page, not the homepage.
**What matters**:

- Pixel and CAPI passing purchase value — non-negotiable.
- With many products — a catalog and dynamic ads: Meta shows people the products they viewed.
- Creatives: product in use, unboxing, reviews, before/after comparisons.
- Watch margin, not just ROAS: returns and shipping eat profit.

**Typical mistake**: optimizing for clicks because "there are too few purchases". Better to merge ad sets and budget for purchases (see [Basics](01-basics.md)).

## Booked services (salon, photographer, craftsperson, rentals)
**Objective**: Leads or messages.
**Send people to**: a form, online booking or Direct/WhatsApp.
**What matters**:

- Reply speed matters more than the ads (see [Ads that open a chat](messaging.md)).
- Show the process and result, the faces of your team, reviews.
- Add a question about date and budget to the form to filter out random leads.
- Watch your capacity: if you're fully booked for two weeks, lower the budget, or you'll pay for people you can't serve.

## Local business (café, neighbourhood shop, studio)
**Objective**: Awareness with reach in your area, or Traffic to the map/profile; Engagement for promotions.
**What matters**:

- Location: city or radius, location expansion off.
- Show a recognisable place in the creative: street, sign, interior. Locals should think "that's near me".
- Measuring is hard: use an "Instagram" promo code or ask customers.

## Events (concert, workshop, fair)
**Objective**: Ticket sales or Leads (registration).
**What matters**:

- Launch 3–6 weeks ahead, not 5 days: learning takes time.
- Lifetime budget ending on the event day.
- Closer to the date — reminders for people who viewed the page but didn't buy.
- Honest urgency: "20 seats left" only if true.

## Health and medicine (clinics, doctors, rehabilitation)
**Objective**: Leads via instant forms or messages.
**What matters**:

- Special restrictions apply: the robot may not see website leads (see [Restrictions](07-restrictions.md)).
- Copy without addressing conditions ("Does your … hurt?") and without promising results.
- Show people, equipment, doctors and the process, not before/after.
- Never put the pixel in a patient portal.

## B2B (services for businesses)
**Objective**: Leads (higher-intent forms) + CRM connection.
**What matters**:

- Long sales cycles mean Ads Manager underreports: check against your CRM.
- Useful content works: a checklist, a breakdown, a mini-course in exchange for contact details.
- Check whether LinkedIn or Google would be better (see [Choosing a platform](platforms.md)).
