# Reading reports in Ads Manager

Ads are live, numbers are coming in. Now the key is not to drown in a hundred columns. Here's where things are and what to look at.

## The Ads Manager table: three tabs
At the top are three tabs, like the three floors of an ad: **Campaigns**, **Ad sets**, **Ads**. Tick a campaign and the next tabs show only what's inside it.

Rule: make creative decisions on the **Ads** tab, budget and audience decisions on the **Ad sets** tab.

## Four tools you need every day
**Date range**
: top right. For evaluation use the last 7 or 14 days, not "today": one day's numbers jump around. The **Compare** toggle there shows the change versus the previous period.

**Columns**
: the set of metrics. There are presets ("Performance", "Engagement"), but it's better to build your own: "Columns" → "Customize columns" → tick what you need → "Save as preset".

**Breakdown**
: the button next to "Columns". It splits results by age, gender, country, region, placement, device, day. This is where you see that money went to Audience Network or another city.

**Filters and search**
: to quickly find active campaigns or ads with a certain word in the name.

## A starter column set
8–10 columns are enough. More is noise.

| Column | Why |
|---|---|
| Delivery | Is it running, learning, rejected? |
| Amount spent | How much money went out |
| Results | How many target actions (purchases, leads) |
| Cost per result | The key number to compare with your limit |
| Impressions and reach | How many times and to how many people |
| Frequency | Are people tired of the ad? |
| CPM | What impressions cost |
| CTR (link click-through rate) | Does the ad grab attention? |
| Quality ranking | How Meta rates the ad compared to competitors |

## The most useful breakdowns
- **By placement** — where money is spent and where people buy. Feed, Reels, Stories, Audience Network.
- **By age and gender** — who actually buys and who only clicks.
- **By region** — did the ad drift to another city or country?
- **By day** — how results changed over time.

!!! warning "The breakdown catch"
    Some breakdowns (e.g. region or hour) don't show website purchases — you'll see zeros or "Uncategorised". Look at purchases in the total row or in a breakdown that supports them (age, gender, country, placement).

## How to read: the order
1. **Delivery**: is everything running? Any rejected ads?
2. **Cost per result** versus your limit ([unit economics](01-basics.md)).
3. If it's expensive, walk the **CPM → CTR → conversion** chain and find the weak link.
4. **Frequency** and **CTR** per ad — any fatigue?
5. **Placement breakdown** — any "black hole"?

## Saved reports
In the "Reports" menu you can build a report with your own columns and breakdowns, save it, export it as a file, share it via link or get it by email on a schedule. Handy if you report to someone or want weekly results without opening Ads Manager.

## What not to do
- Don't make decisions on one day of data.
- Don't compare campaigns with different objectives: "Traffic" and "Sales" have different norms.
- Remember that Ads Manager shows Meta's data. Check real sales against your shop or CRM ([Tracking](04-tracking.md)).
