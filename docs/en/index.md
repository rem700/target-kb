# Targeted ads without the boring bits

A knowledge base for people who have heard of "targeted ads" but aren't quite sure what goes on inside. Up to date as of November 2026. Mostly about Instagram and Facebook ads (both owned by Meta), with a bit on TikTok.

## Targeted ads in 30 seconds
You pay a social network to show your post to people who don't follow you yet. The network knows a lot about its users and tries to show your ad to the ones most likely to buy. Your job: give it a good ad, tell it what counts as success, and don't get in the way while it learns.

## Meet Anya
One example runs through this whole base. Anya makes handmade candles and sells them on her website. She has an Instagram account, a small ad budget and no ad specialist friends. Everything below, we'll try on her.

## Five ideas worth remembering
!!! tip "If you only read this"
    1. **A robot picks the audience now.** Specialists used to hand-pick "women 25–35 who like yoga". Today the algorithm does it better than a human.
    2. **Your ad is your audience setting.** The robot looks at the image and text and works out who to show them to.
    3. **Simple beats complicated.** One or two campaigns with many different videos work better than a clever setup.
    4. **The robot learns from your data.** If it can't see who bought, it learns blindfolded.
    5. **Trust the till, not pretty charts.** The number that matters is how many real customers came in.
